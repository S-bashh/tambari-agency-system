THIS AGREEMENT is made on the {{date_long}} between {{landlord_name}} of {{landlord_address}} (hereinafter referred to as "THE LANDLORD") which expression shall where the context so admits or requires include his/her successor-in-title and assigns of the first part AND {{tenant_name}} of {{tenant_address}} (hereinafter referred to as "THE TENANT") which expression shall where the context so admits or requires include his/her administrators or assigns of the other part.

WHEREAS: The Landlord is the owner of the property situated at {{property_address}} ({{property_title}}, ref. {{property_ref}}) and the Tenant occupies the property for a tenancy period of {{duration_text}} certain in consideration of the sum of {{rent_amount}} ({{rent_words}}) {{rent_frequency_text}}.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

In consideration of the rent and the Tenant's covenants hereinafter reserved and contained, the Landlord demises unto the Tenant ALL THAT property at {{property_address}} (hereinafter called "the Demised Premises") to hold unto the Tenant for the term of {{duration_text}} from {{start_date_long}} to {{end_date_long}}.

TENANT'S COVENANTS WITH THE LANDLORD ARE AS FOLLOWS:

  1. To pay the said rent at the time and in the manner hereinbefore mentioned.
  2. To keep the Demised Premises clean, and in good and tenantable state of repairs and condition.
  3. To use the Demised Premises for {{purpose}} purposes only.
  4. Not to assign, sublet or part with the possession of the Demised Premises or any part thereof without the consent of the Landlord.
  5. To pay for electricity bills, water rates, waste disposal, security and all other amenities consumed on the Demised Premises.
  6. To keep all the fittings, fixtures and appurtenances in good state of repairs at all times during this tenancy.
  7. To pay all expenses incurred or liable to be incurred in case of damages to the Demised Premises or fittings, fixtures and appurtenances attached thereto.
  8. To allow the Landlord or his/her agents at all reasonable times during the pendency of this tenancy to enter upon and inspect the Demised Premises, subject to reasonable notice thereof being given to the Tenant by the Landlord.
  9. Not to do or permit to be done on the Demised Premises any act or thing which is or may cause annoyance or nuisance to the Landlord or the occupiers of any adjoining premises/houses.
  10. Not to make or permit to be made any major alteration or additions to the Demised Premises without the consent of the Landlord (installation of air conditioning units excepted).
  11. To yield up the Demised Premises at the end of the tenancy, unless renewed, with all the fixtures and fittings in good condition.
  12. That where the Tenant decides to renew, he/she shall give to the Landlord notice of his/her intention to renew, followed by payment of the agreed rent prior to the expiration of the current term.

THE LANDLORD HEREBY COVENANTS WITH THE TENANT AS FOLLOWS:

  1. That the Tenant shall peaceably hold and enjoy the property during the term without any interruption or interference by the Landlord or any person rightfully claiming under or in trust for the Landlord.
  2. That where the present agreement is determined for any reason contained in this agreement, the Landlord may cause a quantity surveyor to value any damages done in the premises by the Tenant and where the Tenant cannot repair the damages done, he/she shall pay to the Landlord whatever value of the damages done as assessed by the quantity surveyor.
  3. Not to review or attempt to review the rent payable on the Demised Premises without giving or serving on the Tenant a Notice of Intention to Review at least three (3) months prior to the expiration of the current term.

PROVIDED ALWAYS and it is agreed and declared as follows:

  1. That if the rent reserved or any part of it shall be unpaid for twenty-one (21) days after becoming payable, whether formally demanded or not; or if any covenant on the part of the Tenant contained herein is not performed or observed; or if the Tenant shall become bankrupt or enter into any composition with his/her creditors or suffer any distress or execution upon his/her goods, then it shall be lawful for the Landlord at any time thereafter to re-enter upon the property or any part thereof and the tenancy shall absolutely cease.
  2. That all notices required to be given under the terms of this tenancy shall be in writing and, if addressed to the Tenant, shall be sufficiently served if left in the premises occupied by the Tenant or sent to the Tenant by registered post; and any notice addressed to the Landlord shall be sufficiently served if left at the Landlord's last known address or served on any agent authorised by the Landlord to receive it or who on the Landlord's behalf collected the rent of the premises.
